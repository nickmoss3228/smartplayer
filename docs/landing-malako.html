Difficulty, measured the way milk is

Levels are graded by fat content

Three levels, and each one is thicker than the last. You start on skimmed and work up to something you have to chew.

1%Milk fat

Easy — foundational

Present simple, everyday routine, one speaker at a conversational pace. Short enough to replay end to end while you are still holding the whole thing in your head.

2.5%Milk fat

Medium — intermediate

Longer arcs, past and future tenses in the same breath, and the first phrasal verbs that mean nothing like the words they are made of.

6%Milk fat

Hard — advanced

Full pace, contractions swallowed whole, clauses stacked three deep. This is where drill mode stops being optional.
